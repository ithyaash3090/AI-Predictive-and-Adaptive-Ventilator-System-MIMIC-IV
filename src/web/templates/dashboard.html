Clinical Safety Constraints & Ethical Governance Architecture

ARDSNet & IEC 60601 Medical Safety Standards

1. Explicit Safety Constraints Envelope

The adaptive controller is strictly bounded by hard clinical limits developed from consensus ICU guidelines:

  • Tidal Volume Bound: Strictly clamped between 4.0 and 8.0 mL/kg Predicted Body Weight (PBW). Initial target 6.0 mL/kg PBW.
  • Barotrauma Ceiling: Plateau Pressure ($P_{\text{plat}}$) never permitted to exceed 30 cmH2O.
  • Driving Pressure Limit: Driving pressure ($\Delta P = P_{\text{plat}} - \text{PEEP}$) targeted strictly ≤ 14 cmH2O.
  • Rate-of-Change Limiters: Maximum step change per cycle: $\Delta\text{FiO}_2 \le \pm 0.10$, $\Delta\text{PEEP} \le \pm 2.0\text{ cmH}_2\text{O}$, $\Delta V_t \le \pm 50\text{ mL}$.
  • Hyperoxia Prevention: De-escalation enforced when $\text{SpO}_2 > 96\%$ on $\text{FiO}_2 > 0.30$.

2. Clinician-in-the-Loop Supervision

Autonomous execution is restricted by design to controlled research simulation. In clinical deployment:

  • Tiered Supervision: Low-risk micro-adjustments generate passive suggestions; significant titrations require affirmative clinician sign-off.
  • Instant Override: Clinicians retain absolute manual override authority with single-click fallback to conventional modes.
  • Complete Auditability: Every proposed adjustment is logged with clinical rationale, predictive risk score, and safety audit verification.
  • Alert Fatigue Mitigation: Machine learning ensemble achieves >96% specificity, eliminating unnecessary nuisance alarms.
